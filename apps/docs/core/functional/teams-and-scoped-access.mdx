---
title: Teams and scoped project access
description: Give members access to specific projects with access teams, scoped invitations, and direct grants.
---

By default a workspace member reaches every project in the workspace. When that is too broad, Kaneo scopes a member to a set of projects — through an **access team**, a **scoped invitation**, or a **direct grant**. The workspace stays the authorization boundary; project scope narrows what a member can see and do inside it.

## Roles

Members have a workspace role: **owner**, **admin**, **member**, or **viewer**. The role defines the permission set; project scope decides which projects those permissions apply to.

## Invite someone with a scope

1. Open the workspace **Members** page and choose **Invite member**.
2. Enter the email, pick a role, and set the **scope**: all projects or a selection of projects, optionally through one or more access teams.
3. Send the invitation. Pending invitations stay listed and can be cancelled until accepted; the scope is materialised when the invitation is accepted and follows later team edits dynamically.

To invite without an email, create a **shareable invite link** with an expiry (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or never) and an optional maximum number of uses. Revoke a link at any time.

## Access teams

Access teams are named bundles of projects and members, managed from **Settings → Workspace → Teams**. Use them when several people should share the same project set — for example a client pod or a squad.

- A team has a name, an optional description, members, and a project scope (all projects or a selection).
- Adding a member to a team grants that member the team's projects; removing them takes the grant back.
- Editing the team's project scope updates access for every member.

## Direct grants on an existing member

On the Members page, open a member's menu and choose **Manage access**. You can grant individual projects or lift the scope back to full workspace access. A scoped member keeps access to a project they create themselves through a direct grant.

## What scoping affects

A scoped member only sees and touches their projects across the product: project lists and boards, assignable members, global search results, notifications, project WebSockets, and private assets. Permission checks remain the same; they are simply evaluated against the projects the member can reach.
